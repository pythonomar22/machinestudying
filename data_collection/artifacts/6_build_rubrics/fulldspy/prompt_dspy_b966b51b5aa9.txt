- Question: `I'm not trying to thread a whole batch; I want to `await` many DSPy program calls inside my own async code. What does DSPy's async wrapper preserve, and what actually limits its concurrency?`
- Gold answer:
`dspy.asyncify` captures the current DSPy context at call time, runs the wrapped callable in worker threads, and restores those overrides inside the worker before invoking the program. Concurrency is throttled by a process-global `CapacityLimiter` whose size tracks `dspy.settings.async_max_workers` (default `8`). The limiter object is reused across calls; only its token count changes when the setting changes.
