- Label: `evaluation_metrics_and_parallelism`
- Question: `On long evaluations, I want timed-out tail tasks retried without late duplicates overwriting completed work. What do DSPy's parallel executors actually do with stragglers and retries?`
- Gold answer:
Timeout-based retry only exists in the parallel path. DSPy checks for stragglers only when the unfinished set has shrunk to `straggler_limit` or fewer futures, and only after each `wait(..., timeout=1, return_when=FIRST_COMPLETED)` cycle. Any overdue unfinished future is resubmitted as a new future. The retry guard is per future object, not per logical item, so a retry can itself be retried on a later cycle. Late duplicate completions do not overwrite an earlier result because a completion stores its outcome only while `results[index] is None`. Setting `timeout=0` disables straggler retries.
