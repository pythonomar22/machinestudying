`dspy.ReAct` builds two different predictors. The planning predictor starts from your original task instructions, then appends agent/tool/trajectory boilerplate and synthetic outputs like `next_thought`, `next_tool_name`, and `next_tool_args`. The final extractor keeps the original task instructions, adds `trajectory` as an input, and then `dspy.ChainOfThought` prepends a `reasoning` output field.

def weather(city: str) -> str:

class QA(dspy.Signature):
    question: str = dspy.InputField()
    answer: str = dspy.OutputField()
