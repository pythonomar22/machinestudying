- Question: `If parallel worker tasks each enter their own `dspy.context(...)`, do those LM or settings overrides leak across worker threads or back into the main thread?`
- Gold answer:
No. `ParallelExecutor` copies the parent thread-local overrides into each worker before running the task, and resets them when the worker finishes. A nested `dspy.context(...)` inside one task is therefore local to that worker, and the main thread keeps its original settings after the parallel call returns.
