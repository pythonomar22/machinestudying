- Gold answer:
`dspy.Evaluate` can aggregate it directly. The per-example metric object is kept in `results`, and aggregation works because `dspy.Prediction` supports numeric conversion and addition through its `score` field. So a metric can return `dspy.Prediction(score=..., feedback=...)`; `Evaluate` will still compute the overall score, and the feedback remains accessible on each stored metric result.
